## S1: Few users hit the weekly cap

- **Claim:** Anthropic said its new weekly limits would affect "less than 5% of users based on current usage patterns" (Anthropic's email to subscribers, as quoted by the article).
- **Source:** Clinton Stark, "Anthropic adds weekly limits to Claude, cites abuses", Stark Insider, 28 July 2025. https://www.starkinsider.com/2025/07/anthropic-adds-weekly-limits-to-claude-cites-abuses.html
- **Checked:** 2026-10-17 (quote read from the article).
- **What it supports:** most users never reach the cap.
- **What it does NOT say:** how much of their allowance people actually use, or how much goes unused.
- **Caveat:** from 2025; limits and usage may have changed. Secondary source quoting Anthropic's email, not an Anthropic page.
- **Pitch wording:** "When Anthropic introduced weekly limits in 2025, it said they would affect less than 5% of users, so most people never reach their cap." Do not say "most people use under half".
